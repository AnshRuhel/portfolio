01 / About

Design instincts. Engineering discipline.

Two backgrounds, one output — products that look considered and work properly.

I started as a graphic designer and moved into full-stack development because I got tired of handing off polished mockups and watching them lose their edge in the build. Now I do both — which means fewer handoffs, fewer misunderstandings, and interfaces that actually match the vision.

Lately I've been going deep on AI & machine learning, exploring how intelligent features can be built into practical software rather than bolted on as a gimmick. I also work the business side — B2B software and B2B marketing — so I think about positioning, not just pixels and code.

I collaborate on other people's projects more than I run my own — I like walking into a problem that's already got shape and helping sharpen it.
